



## *Global Music Culture*

### • Music Cognition, Cross-Cultural
### • Big data, Machine Learning
!(/images/global_music_culture.png?aspect=1.8181)

How has popular music evolved across time and space? Have modern technology and the internet changed the way we consume and share music?

Until recently, these questions were hard to answer because of limited data and analytical tools. The digital age and advances in machine learning have opened new ways to study music scientifically.

Throughout my PhD, I assembled a dataset of some 250,000 popular songs from around the globe, spanning the 1950s to the present. This collection is complemented by an ongoing radio monitoring project covering 220 countries, which has already accumulated tens of terabytes of audio of spoken conversation and music.

Applying machine learning tools to this library, including music information retrieval, natural language processing, and social network analysis, my collaborators and I characterise the global history of popular music through a data-driven approach.
