



## *Cultural Spread*

### • Contagion, Diffusion
### • Network, Online Experiments
!(/images/cultural_spread.png?aspect=1.8181)

From gossip about your peers to viral TikToks that spread across society, patterns of contagion follow similar rules. By knowing which musical trends catch on where and when, we can make probabilistic estimates about musical connections between places, and ultimately predict patterns of spread.

Tools like *PsyNet* also let us study these scenarios experimentally. Picture an online version of the Chinese whispers game, where we can track exactly how messages change as they pass between people. We can even create miniature virtual societies to observe how the breadth of interactions between people shapes group behaviour.

By combining these experiments with real-world data, we are beginning to understand how ideas and cultural trends catch on and spread.
