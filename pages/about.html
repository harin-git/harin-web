I'm interested in decoding cultural patterns, from the aesthetic choices we make as individuals to how culture evolves and transmits across cities and countries. I study music to understand these patterns, gathering hundreds of thousands of songs from around the world, from archival recordings to online streaming data, and applying machine learning and network analysis to analyse them. I combine this big data with behavioural experiments, running cross-cultural studies online using PsyNet and making field trips (such as travelling to the Bolivian Amazon).

I'm a Junior Research Fellow at King's College, University of Cambridge and co-direct the Music, Cognition and Culture (MCC) group at the Centre for Music and Science. I earned my PhD in Cognitive Science from the Max Planck Institutes in Germany (MPI CBS and MPIEA), supervised by Dr Nori Jacoby, Dr Marc Schönwiesner, and Dr Minsu Park.
